LXGWWenKai-Regular.woff2 is the complete official Regular font converted
from TTF to WOFF2 with fontTools. LXGWWenKai-Common.woff2 is a subset for
characters present in this site's initial templates and documents.
The full font remains available as an automatic fallback for future content.
No glyph designs were changed. Comic Sans MS is resolved locally and is
not redistributed in this repository.
